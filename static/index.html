Asset Declaration

Welcome message

Rwanda Revenue Authority are aware mandate of tax collection on behalf of the government of Rwanda, and to assist its members. It has an obligation to fight against corruption and other misconducts. According to Article 8 of RRA Code of Conduct on Declaration of Assets, all RRA employees have an obligation to declare their assets annually.

What do you have to know?

RRA employees are obliged to submit their personal assets, the assets of their spouses and those of their children below the age of 18. You should provide all required information on your assets both in Rwanda and abroad.
